The Physio_AI system is an advanced artificial intelligence tool designed by a team of medical engineers in 2024. Its primary function is to analyze physical therapy movements and provide real-time feedback to patients recovering from knee surgery. 

Physio_AI uses a novel sensor mesh called the "FlexiMesh", which is worn around the joint. The system relies on a proprietary algorithm known as the "Kinematic-Sync Engine" to detect micro-deviations in the patient's form, predicting potential re-injury with 98% accuracy.

Dr. Aris Thorne is the lead researcher on the project, and the first clinical trials were conducted at the Nova Medical Institute in Berlin. The project was initially funded by the HealthTech Innovators Grant, receiving a record-breaking $15 million in seed funding.

In late 2025, a major software update called "Version 2.0 Catalyst" was released for Physio_AI. This update expanded the system's capabilities to include shoulder and elbow rehabilitation. A new feature called "Haptic-Guidance" was introduced, which uses gentle vibrations in the FlexiMesh to physically correct a patient's posture in real-time. The company also announced a partnership with Global Sports Corp to bring Physio_AI to professional athletes worldwide.
